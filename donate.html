What your donation pays for

This isn't just software on a screen. To make a diagnostic function work, I need to connect to actual modules, capture traffic, compare results, fix the code and test it again.

  • Buy used ECUs and body modules to test safely on the bench
  • Build harnesses, breakout connections and power setups
  • Capture USB / Bluetooth / CAN communications and verify responses
  • Repeat testing on more vehicles and manufacturers
  • Turn working results into stable app updates and clear documentation

Important: Donating supports development; it does not purchase app activation, guarantee a release date, or unlock unfinished vehicle functions.

Development workflow

What actually needs to be done.

A working app isn't just a menu full of buttons. Every useful feature needs a successful hardware transaction and a tested result.

01

Bench setups & donor ECUs

Find modules, identify pinouts, wire CAN/K-line and power safely. Record the exact hardware used in each test.

Ongoing
02

V200 Bluetooth & USB communication

Verify connection, adapter initialization, session handling, packet parsing, timeouts and repeatable diagnostic responses.

In active development
03

Read ECU ID, VIN and fault codes

Confirm supported protocols and modules, then validate IDs, faults, clear-fault behavior and vehicle coverage against the real car.

Testing varies by vehicle
04

Topology, reports & live data

Connect scan results to usable screens; confirm each module shown is based on an actual response rather than a placeholder.

Under development
05

Advanced tests and immobilizer functions

Research ECU-specific commands and required authorization; bench-validate before enabling any write, routine or key-programming action.

Not broadly validated
06

Ship, document & retest

Publish Android builds, share the actual limitations, collect confirmed test results and fix regressions before expanding coverage.

Straight answers

A few important things.

Does donating activate an app?

No. Donations support development only. App activation and any subscription are handled separately.

Is the donation counter live?

The progress bar checks for updates every 15 seconds. Verified PayPal donations can be added automatically after the secure PayPal webhook service is connected; until then the recorded total is updated by the owner.

Are advanced ECU functions already finished?

No. Compatibility differs by module and version. Advanced and immobilizer actions must be verified on suitable test hardware before release.
