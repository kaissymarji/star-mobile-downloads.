# EEPROM Studio 1.0.4 — CH341A suspicious-dump warning (Experimental Beta)

This signed 1.0.4 update adds safety warnings for CH341A read-only EEPROM dumps, without changing XP400 hardware operations.

- Warns when CH341A produces an all-FF dump, an all-00 dump, or an unusually long FF prefix.
- Offers **Keep flagged dump** or **Discard and reseat adapter**. Flagged data is not silently accepted or exported before confirmation.
- Shows checks for adapter slot, notch/pin-1 alignment, contact seating and voltage.
- Keeps all XP400 read/write, MCU and key/transponder behavior unchanged.

**Important:** An EEPROM can legitimately contain only FF or 00. The warning is a precaution, not proof of incorrect placement or corrupt data. CH341A read/write functionality has not been physically certified for every chip or adapter; CH341A writing and erase remain disabled. Verify dump data against a trusted programmer where important. Do not write or erase a chip based solely on the warning.
